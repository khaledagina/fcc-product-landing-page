How it works

Focusly is based on the Pomodoro Technique, created by Francesco Cirillo. Here is the technique explained by its creator.

  1. Pick a task. Choose one thing to work on.
  2. Start the timer. Work with no distractions until it rings.
  3. Take a short break. Stand up, drink water, rest your eyes.
  4. Repeat. After four rounds, take a longer break.
